Noticing yellow leaves on your pothos plant? Don't worry! Your plant might just need a little extra care.

Pothos Plant Yellow Leaves can happen for several reasons, including overwatering, poor drainage, insufficient light, or natural aging. The good news? A few simple changes can help your plant thrive again.

Check your watering routine: Let the top inch or two of soil dry before watering again.

Provide the right light: Place your pothos near a window with bright, indirect sunlight.

Improve drainage: Use a pot with drainage holes to prevent soggy roots and root rot.

Remove yellow leaves: Trim damaged leaves to keep your plant looking fresh and healthy.
